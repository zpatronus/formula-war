Use the x coordinates on the grid. Your team is always shown on the left, firing toward increasing x. The constant adjusts automatically so the curve passes through your unit. How to play

Enter a room name to create or join it, or choose Practice solo. In multiplayer, pick the red or blue team, and let the host start the match. You can also stay in the room as a spectator.

On your turn, write a function of x. Your projectile follows f(x) + c using the arena grid; c is chosen so the curve passes through your unit. Fire to reveal your curve. Your team appears on the negative side and fires left to right. Blue players see a mirrored board; their equations are mapped to the shared arena by substituting −x for x.

Eliminate the other team to win. Each shot has 600 units of travel, and stops at the first obstacle, undefined region, or arena boundary, including the top and bottom.
